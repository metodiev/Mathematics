# Triangle Trigonometry — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 37 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Work each problem completely. For a triangle, state every side and every angle you find, and state
the case (SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS or SSA) you are using. When the case is SSA, decide how many triangles
exist before solving. Give angles to two decimal places and lengths to three significant figures
unless an exact value is available.

Problems 35 and 36 use the sum-to-product formulas from
[Trigonometric Identities](../03-trigonometric-identities/README.md).

Full solutions are in [solutions.md](solutions.md). Attempt the problems before opening that file.

## Beginner

**1.** In triangle $ABC$, $A=42^\circ$, $B=75^\circ$ and $a=10$. Find $b$.

**2.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=24$, $A=30^\circ$ and $B=45^\circ$. Find $b$.

**3.** In triangle $ABC$, $b=9$, $c=14$ and $A=48^\circ$. Find $a$.

**4.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=5$, $b=8$ and $c=10$. Find the angle $C$.

**5.** Find the area of the triangle with $a=7$, $b=10$ and included angle $C=52^\circ$.

**6.** Find the area of an equilateral triangle of side $6$.

**7.** Find the area of the triangle with sides $6$, $8$ and $10$.

**8.** Find the area of the triangle with sides $9$, $10$ and $17$.

**9.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=13$ and $A=50^\circ$. Find the circumradius $R$.

**10.** In triangle $ABC$, $A=80^\circ$ and $B=40^\circ$. Find $C$ and state which side is the longest.

**11.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=14$, $b=9$ and $A=60^\circ$. Find the angle $B$.

**12.** A point $Q$ is $12$ km from $P$ on a bearing of $070^\circ$, and a point $R$ is $8$ km from
$P$ on a bearing of $160^\circ$. Find the distance $QR$.

## Intermediate

**13.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=6$, $b=12$ and $A=35^\circ$. Show that no such triangle exists.

**14.** In triangle $ABC$, $b=16$, $c=20$ and $A=65^\circ$. Solve the triangle.

**15.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=9$, $b=7$ and $c=5$. Solve the triangle.

**16.** In triangle $ABC$, $A=48^\circ$, $B=57^\circ$ and $c=20$. Solve the triangle.

**17.** Find the area of the triangle with sides $9$, $12$ and $15$.

**18.** Find the area of the triangle with sides $7$, $8$ and $13$.

**19.** Find the circumradius of the triangle with sides $5$, $6$ and $7$.

**20.** A ship sails $12$ km from $P$ on a bearing of $025^\circ$ to $Q$, then $5$ km on a bearing of
$115^\circ$ to $R$. Find the distance $PR$ and the bearing of $R$ from $P$.

**21.** Two forces of $50$ N and $80$ N act at a point with an angle of $120^\circ$ between them.
Find the magnitude of the resultant and its angle with the $80$ N force.

**22.** Two forces of $20$ N and $30$ N act at a point with an angle of $50^\circ$ between them.
Find the magnitude of the resultant and its angle with the $30$ N force.

**23.** A lighthouse $L$ is on a bearing of $055^\circ$ from a port $P$ at a distance of $18$ km. A
ship $S$ is on a bearing of $145^\circ$ from $P$ at a distance of $24$ km. Find the distance $LS$.

**24.** Find the area of the isosceles triangle with sides $10$, $10$ and $12$.

## Advanced

**25.** Prove the law of sines by dropping an altitude from a vertex, treating separately the case in
which the foot of the altitude lies outside the opposite side.

**26.** Prove that $\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=2R$ for a triangle with circumradius $R$, using the inscribed
angle theorem.

**27.** Prove the law of cosines by dropping an altitude from the vertex $A$ to the line $BC$ and
applying the Pythagorean theorem to the two right triangles that result.

**28.** Derive Heron's formula $K=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$ from the law of cosines.

**29.** Prove that the area of a triangle satisfies $K=\dfrac{abc}{4R}$.

**30.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=7$, $b=8$ and $A=30^\circ$. Determine the number of triangles satisfying
these data and solve each of them.

**31.** An aircraft has an airspeed of $200$ km/h on a heading of $070^\circ$. The wind blows at
$40$ km/h from $160^\circ$. Find the ground speed and the track of the aircraft.

**32.** Prove the projection formula
$$a=b\cos C+c\cos B$$
for every triangle, taking care with the signs when an angle is obtuse.

**33.** Prove that in every triangle
$$a^2+b^2+c^2=2\bigl(ab\cos C+bc\cos A+ca\cos B\bigr).$$

## Challenge

**34.** In triangle $ABC$, $a=2x$, $b=x+1$, $c=3$ and $C=60^\circ$. Find $x$, and verify that the
resulting sides satisfy the triangle inequality.

**35.** Prove that in every triangle
$$\frac{a+b}{c}=\frac{\cos\dfrac{A-B}{2}}{\sin\dfrac{C}{2}}.$$

**36.** Suppose that $A=2B$ in triangle $ABC$. Prove that
$$a^2=b(b+c).$$

**37.** A triangle has sides $5$, $7$ and $8$. Compute its area $K$, its inradius $r$ and its
circumradius $R$, and verify both $K=rs$ and $abc=4RK$.
