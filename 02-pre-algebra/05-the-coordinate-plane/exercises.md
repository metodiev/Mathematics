# The Coordinate Plane — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

For graphing problems, list the intercepts and a third point, then describe how the line passes through them. For word problems, state the meaning of the slope and intercepts with units. Problems 29–32 require justification, not just a numerical answer.

## Beginner

**1.** State the quadrant of each point: $(3,-5)$, $(-2,4)$, $(-3,-1)$, $(4,6)$.

**2.** Where does the point $(0,-3)$ lie? Is it in a quadrant?

**3.** Write the coordinates of the point $3$ units right and $2$ units down from the origin.

**4.** Find the distance between $(2,1)$ and $(2,7)$.

**5.** Find the distance between $(-1,3)$ and $(4,3)$.

**6.** Find the distance from $(0,0)$ to $(3,4)$.

**7.** Find the midpoint of $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.

**8.** Find the midpoint of $(-3,5)$ and $(1,-1)$.

**9.** Find the slope of the line through $(1,2)$ and $(3,8)$.

**10.** Find the slope of the line through $(-2,5)$ and $(2,-3)$.

**11.** For $y=2x+1$, find $y$ when $x=3$, and state the $y$-intercept.

## Intermediate

**12.** Find the slope of the horizontal line through $(2,5)$ and $(7,5)$, and of the vertical line through $(3,2)$ and $(3,9)$.

**13.** Find the distance between $(-2,-3)$ and $(4,5)$.

**14.** For the segment with endpoints $(-4,1)$ and $(2,9)$, find the midpoint and the length.

**15.** For $y=-\frac12x+3$, state the slope, the $y$-intercept, and the $x$-intercept.

**16.** Rewrite $3x+2y=12$ in slope-intercept form and state the slope and $y$-intercept.

**17.** Write the equation of the line with slope $3$ and $y$-intercept $(0,2)$.

**18.** Write the equation of the line through $(2,1)$ and $(4,7)$.

**19.** Find the $x$- and $y$-intercepts of $4x-2y=8$.

**20.** Is the point $(3,5)$ on the line $y=2x-1$? Justify.

**21.** For $f(x)=5x-3$, compute $f(0)$, $f(-2)$ and $f\left(\frac12\right)$.

## Advanced

**22.** Find the equation of the line through $(1,2)$ that is perpendicular to $y=2x+3$.

**23.** A line passes through the points in the table. Find its equation and predict $y$ when $x=10$.

| $x$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | $4$ | $7$ | $10$ |

**24.** A rental company charges $C(n)=25+8n$ dollars for $n$ hours. Interpret the slope and the $y$-intercept with units, and find the number of hours costing \$81.

**25.** Find the distance between $(-3,4)$ and $(5,-2)$.

**26.** Find the midpoint of $\left(\frac12,-3\right)$ and $\left(\frac52,7\right)$.

**27.** Find all values of $k$ such that the point $(4,k)$ is $5$ units from $(1,1)$.

**28.** Is the relation $\{(1,2),(1,5),(3,4)\}$ a function? Explain. Then, for $f(x)=3x+1$, solve $f(x)=16$.

## Challenge

**29.** The vertices of a quadrilateral are $A(0,0)$, $B(4,0)$, $C(5,3)$ and $D(1,3)$. Show that $ABCD$ is a parallelogram by comparing slopes. Show that its diagonals bisect each other by comparing midpoints. Is it a rectangle? Justify.

**30.** Find all points on the line $y=2x$ that are exactly $5$ units from the origin.

**31.** A runner's distance from home is recorded as follows: $12$ km after $1$ hour, still $12$ km after the second hour, and $20$ km after the fourth hour. Assuming the motion is piecewise linear, find the slope of each segment, interpret each slope, and compute the average speed over the four hours.

**32.** Show that $A(-1,1)$, $B(3,5)$ and $C(7,9)$ are collinear by comparing slopes, and find the equation of the line containing them.
