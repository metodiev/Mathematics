# Variables and Algebraic Expressions — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

Simplify means: remove parentheses and combine like terms until no rule applies. Justify each non-obvious step by naming the property used — commutative, associative or distributive. For evaluation problems, write the substituted expression with parentheses before computing.

## Beginner

**1.** Translate into an expression: "seven more than a number $n$".

**2.** Translate into an expression: "the product of $5$ and the sum of $x$ and $2$".

**3.** For the expression $7x^2 - 4x + 9$, list the terms, state the coefficient of $x$, and state the constant term.

**4.** Combine like terms: $5x + 3x$.

**5.** Combine like terms: $9y - 4y + 2$.

**6.** Combine like terms: $6a + 2b - 4a + 5b$.

**7.** Distribute: $3(x + 5)$.

**8.** Distribute: $-2(4x - 3)$.

**9.** Evaluate $4x - 7$ at $x = 3$.

**10.** Evaluate $2x^2 + x$ at $x = -3$.

**11.** Evaluate $5(a + b)$ at $a = 2$, $b = -6$.

## Intermediate

**12.** Simplify $8 + 3(x - 2)$.

**13.** Simplify $4(2x + 3) - 5x$.

**14.** Simplify $7 - 2(3x - 4)$.

**15.** Simplify $5x + 3 - (2x - 7)$.

**16.** Simplify $2(3a + 4b) + 3(2a - b)$.

**17.** Evaluate $\dfrac{x+y}{2}$ at $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$, $y = \dfrac{5}{2}$.

**18.** A shop charges \$2.75 per notebook plus a flat \$4 shipping fee. Write an expression for the total cost of $n$ notebooks, and evaluate it at $n = 6$.

**19.** A rectangle has length $x + 5$ and width $x$. Write and simplify an expression for its perimeter.

**20.** Factor: $12x + 18$.

**21.** Factor: $15a - 25$.

## Advanced

**22.** Explain why $5x + 2y$ cannot be simplified to $7xy$. Give two numerical substitutions that show the two expressions are not equivalent.

**23.** Simplify $3(2x - 1) - 2(3x - 4)$. Explain why the result does not contain $x$.

**24.** Evaluate $2x^2 - 3xy + y^2$ at $x = -2$, $y = 3$.

**25.** Two phone plans cost $A = 0.10n + 25$ and $B = 0.08n + 40$ dollars for $n$ minutes. Write and simplify $A - B$. For which $n$ is plan $A$ cheaper?

**26.** Use the distributive property twice to expand $(x+3)(x+2)$, and illustrate the result with an area model split into four rectangles.

**27.** The formula $F = \frac{9}{5}C + 32$ converts Celsius to Fahrenheit. Evaluate $F$ at $C = -40$, $C = 100$ and $C = 37$.

**28.** Prove that $a(b+c) = ab + ac$ for positive whole numbers $a,b,c$ by counting the unit squares of an $a \times (b+c)$ rectangle in two ways.

## Challenge

**29.** (a) Show that $2(x+3)$ and $2x + 6$ are equivalent. (b) Show that $2(x+3)$ and $2x + 3$ are not equivalent. (c) Explain why the equation $2(x+3) = 2x + 3$ can never be true, no matter what $x$ is.

**30.** A pattern of squares is built from matchsticks: the first square uses 4 sticks, and each additional square attached to the right uses 3 more sticks. Write an expression for the number of sticks in a row of $n$ squares. How many squares are in a row that uses 301 sticks? How many sticks are in a row of 40 squares?

**31.** Prove that the sum of two consecutive odd integers is always divisible by $4$. Use expressions for the two integers and simplify the sum.

**32.** Explain why $x^2$ and $2x$ are not equivalent expressions. Then find the two values of $x$ for which they happen to be equal.
