# Order of Operations — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

Work each problem without a calculator first, then check. Show the intermediate line after each precedence level, not only the final answer — the intermediate lines are where errors are caught. Problems 29–32 are open-ended or discussion problems; a complete answer includes the reasoning, not only a final value.

## Beginner

**1.** Compute $8 + 4 \times 3$.

**2.** Compute $25 - 6 \div 2$.

**3.** Compute $3^2 + 4^2$.

**4.** Compute $2 + 3^2 \times 2$.

**5.** Compute $18 \div 3 \times 2$.

**6.** Compute $18 \div (3 \times 2)$.

**7.** Compute $-4^2$.

**8.** Compute $(-4)^2$.

**9.** Compute $\sqrt{64} - \sqrt{9}$.

**10.** Compute $(5 - 2)^3$.

**11.** Compute $4(6 + 2) \div 8$.

## Intermediate

**12.** Compute $7 + 2(9 - 4) \div 5$.

**13.** Compute $\dfrac{15 - 3 \times 4}{2 + 1}$.

**14.** Compute $5 - 2\,[\,3 - (4 - 6)\,]$.

**15.** Compute $\sqrt{100 - 64} \times 3$.

**16.** Compute $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6} \times \dfrac{3}{4}$.

**17.** Evaluate $2x^2 - 3x + 1$ at $x = -2$.

**18.** Evaluate $\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}$ at $a = 5$, $b = -3$.

**19.** Compute $2^3 + 3(4 - 1)^2$.

**20.** Insert one pair of parentheses in $2 + 3 \times 4 - 1$ so that the value becomes $19$.

**21.** True or false: $10 - 4 - 3 = 10 - (4 - 3)$. Justify by computing both sides.

## Advanced

**22.** Compute $3\{\,2 + 4[\,5 - 2(3 - 1)\,]\,\} \div 6$.

**23.** Evaluate $\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{x - y}$ at $x = 5$, $y = -3$. Then simplify $x^2-y^2$ by factoring and explain why the two answers must agree whenever $x \neq y$.

**24.** Compute $2^{3^2}$ and $(2^3)^2$. Are they equal? Explain which convention produces which value.

**25.** Evaluate $6 \div 2(1+2)$ under (a) the left-to-right convention for $\div$ and $\times$, and (b) the convention that juxtaposition binds more tightly than $\div$. Rewrite the expression with parentheses so that each reading is unambiguous.

**26.** Give three numbers $a,b,c$ such that $(a-b)-c \neq a-(b-c)$. Compute both sides.

**27.** Compute $\lvert -8\rvert - 2^2 \times (-3)$.

**28.** Compute $\dfrac{(2+3)^2 - (2^2 + 3^2)}{2 \times 3}$ and explain why the numerator is $2 \times 2 \times 3$.

## Challenge

**29.** Using each of the numbers $3, 3, 8, 8$ exactly once, together with any of $+,-,\times,\div$ and parentheses, construct an expression whose value is $24$.

**30.** Using exactly four $4$s each time (and any operations and parentheses), construct expressions equal to $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$ and $5$. For example, $4 + 4 - 4 - 4 = 0$ handles zero.

**31.** A student writes $2 + 3 \times 4 = 20$. Write the expression the student actually evaluated, and explain in one sentence why the original expression is not $20$.

**32.** Compute
$$\dfrac{-(-2)^3 + (-3)^2}{-2^2 + 1}.$$
Take particular care with the difference between $-2^2$ and $(-2)^2$.
