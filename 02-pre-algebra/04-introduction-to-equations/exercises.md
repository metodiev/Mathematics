# Introduction to Equations — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

Solve each equation and **check** the solution in the original equation. For word problems, begin by defining the variable in words, then write the equation, solve it, and state the answer with units. Problems 29–32 are classification or reasoning problems; a bare answer is not sufficient.

## Beginner

**1.** Solve $x+9=14$.

**2.** Solve $y-7=-2$.

**3.** Solve $6m=54$.

**4.** Solve $\dfrac{t}{4}=12$.

**5.** Solve $-3x=21$.

**6.** Solve $\dfrac{x}{5}=-2$.

**7.** Solve $8+x=3$.

**8.** Solve $2x=1$.

**9.** Solve $3x+4=19$.

**10.** Solve $5x-7=18$.

**11.** Is $x=3$ a solution of $4x-5=7$? Justify by substitution.

## Intermediate

**12.** Solve $2x+9=3$.

**13.** Solve $7-3x=1$.

**14.** Solve $\dfrac{x}{3}+5=2$.

**15.** Solve $4(x-2)=20$.

**16.** Solve $-2(x+3)=10$.

**17.** Solve $5x-3=2x+9$.

**18.** Solve $3x+8=5x-2$.

**19.** Solve $2(x+1)=3x-4$.

**20.** Solve $\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{x}{3}=10$.

**21.** Solve $0.4x-1.2=0.2$.

## Advanced

**22.** Solve $\dfrac{2}{3}x-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{6}$.

**23.** Solve $\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac{x+3}{5}$.

**24.** Solve $0.08x+0.12(300-x)=30$.

**25.** Solve $3(2x-1)-2(x+4)=5x-11$.

**26.** Solve the formula $P=2(l+w)$ for $w$.

**27.** The sum of three consecutive even integers is $84$. Find the integers.

**28.** The length of a rectangle is $3$ cm more than twice its width, and the perimeter is $54$ cm. Find the length and width.

## Challenge

**29.** Classify each equation as having one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions. Justify each answer.
(a) $3(x+2)=3x+6$
(b) $2x+5=2x-1$

**30.** Solve $ax+b=cx+d$ for $x$ in terms of $a,b,c,d$. State separately what happens when $a=c$, distinguishing $b=d$ from $b\neq d$.

**31.** Consider $4x+k=3(x+2)+x$. For which values of $k$ does the equation have no solution? For which values does it have infinitely many solutions? Can it have exactly one solution?

**32.** A tank is $\frac13$ full. After $8$ litres are added, it is $\frac34$ full. Find the capacity of the tank.
