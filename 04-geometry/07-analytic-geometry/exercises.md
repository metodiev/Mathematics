# Analytic Geometry and Conic Sections — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Work each problem completely: state the form you are using, show the algebra, and list the elements
(centre or vertex, axes, foci, directrices, asymptotes, eccentricity) before sketching. Full solutions
are in [solutions.md](solutions.md); do not open that file until you have attempted the problems.

## Beginner

**1.** Write $y=5x^2$ in the standard form $x^2=4py$. Give $p$, the focus and the directrix.

**2.** For $y^2=12x$ find $p$, the focus, the directrix and the length of the latus rectum.

**3.** A parabola has vertex at the origin, opens upward and has focus $(0,3)$. Find its equation and its
directrix.

**4.** For $\dfrac{x^2}{36}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$ find $a$, $b$, $c$, the eccentricity, the foci and the
directrices.

**5.** For $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{25}=1$ state the direction of the major axis, then find the foci and
the eccentricity.

**6.** For $\dfrac{x^2}{49}-\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ find the vertices, foci, asymptotes and eccentricity.

**7.** For $\dfrac{y^2}{16}-\dfrac{x^2}{25}=1$ find the vertices, foci, asymptotes and eccentricity.

**8.** Put $4x^2+9y^2=36$ in standard form and list its centre, semi-axes, foci and eccentricity.

**9.** A parabola has vertex at the origin, opens downward and has focus $(0,-2)$. Find its equation and
its directrix.

**10.** An ellipse has semi-major axis $a=10$ and focal distance $c=6$. Find its equation (major axis
horizontal, centre at the origin) and its eccentricity.

**11.** A hyperbola has vertices $(\pm3,0)$ and asymptotes $y=\pm2x$. Find its equation.

**12.** For the circle $x^2+y^2-6x+8y=0$ find the centre and radius by completing the square, then
classify the equation with the discriminant.

## Intermediate

**13.** Put $y^2-6y+8x+25=0$ in standard form. Give the vertex, focus, directrix and axis.

**14.** Identify and describe $4x^2+9y^2-16x+18y-11=0$: centre, semi-axes, foci, eccentricity,
directrices.

**15.** Identify and describe $4x^2-9y^2+16x-18y-29=0$: centre, vertices, foci, asymptotes,
eccentricity.

**16.** A parabola has focus $(1,1)$ and directrix $x=-3$. Find its vertex and its equation.

**17.** An ellipse has foci $(\pm4,0)$ and eccentricity $\tfrac23$. Find its equation.

**18.** A hyperbola has foci $(0,\pm13)$ and eccentricity $\tfrac{13}{5}$. Find its equation and its
asymptotes.

**19.** Classify $2x^2-3xy+4y^2-x+1=0$ without rotating the axes.

**20.** An ellipse has semi-major axis $a=5$ and latus rectum of length $6$. Find $b$, $c$ and $e$.

**21.** Show that $x^2+y^2+2x-4y+8=0$ has no real points, and explain which degenerate case of the
classification this is.

**22.** For $y=x^2-4x+7$ find the vertex, the focal distance, the focus and the directrix.

**23.** Find the value of $k$ for which the line $y=x+k$ is tangent to the parabola $y^2=8x$, and find the
point of tangency.

**24.** A parabolic dish has diameter $2\ \mathrm{m}$ and focal length $0.75\ \mathrm{m}$ (distance from
vertex to focus). How deep is the dish?

**25.** An ellipse is drawn with two pins $8\ \mathrm{cm}$ apart and a string of length $20\ \mathrm{cm}$
looped around them. Find $a$, $c$, $b$ and the equation of the ellipse, taking the pins on the $x$-axis
and the origin at the centre.

## Advanced

**26.** Derive the equation $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ from the condition
$|PF_1|+|PF_2|=2a$ with foci $(\pm c,0)$, and show that $a^2=b^2+c^2$.

**27.** Derive the equation $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ from the condition
$\bigl||PF_1|-|PF_2|\bigr|=2a$ with foci $(\pm c,0)$, and show that $c^2=a^2+b^2$.

**28.** Prove the reflection property of the parabola $y^2=4px$: the tangent at a point $P$ makes equal
angles with the segment $PF$ and with the line through $P$ parallel to the axis.

**29.** Find the locus of points $P$ whose distance to $F=(2,0)$ is **twice** their distance to the line
$x=1$. Identify the conic, write it in standard form with its centre, semi-axes and asymptotes, and verify
that its eccentricity is $2$.

**30.** Derive $r=\dfrac{k}{1+e\cos\theta}$ from the focus–directrix definition with the focus at the pole
and the directrix $x=d$. Then identify the conic with $e=\tfrac35$, $k=\tfrac{16}{5}$: find its
semi-axes, its apsidal distances and its polar equation.

**31.** Prove that $B^2-4AC$ is unchanged by a rotation of axes, that is $B'^2-4A'C'=B^2-4AC$.

**32.** For the family $x^2+Bxy+4y^2=1$, classify the curve for every real $B$, and describe exactly what
happens at the transition values.

## Challenge

**33.** Prove the reflection (optical) property of the ellipse: at every point $P$ of
$\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$, the normal line bisects the angle between $PF_1$ and $PF_2$.
Deduce that a ray from one focus reflects to the other.

**34.** Prove, using Dandelin spheres, that the intersection of a cone with a plane cutting one nappe is an
ellipse, and identify its foci.

**35.** Prove the diameter property of the parabola $y^2=4px$: the midpoints of all chords of slope
$m\ne0$ lie on the line $y=\dfrac{2p}{m}$. What is the locus when $m=0$?

**36.** A comet has perihelion $0.6\ \mathrm{AU}$ and aphelion $5.4\ \mathrm{AU}$. (a) Find the semi-major
axis $a$, the focal distance $c$ and the eccentricity $e$. (b) Using Kepler's third law $T^2=a^3$ in
astronomical units and years, find its orbital period. (c) Write its polar equation with the Sun at the
focus and give the perihelion and aphelion directions.
