# Circles — Exercises

> Companion to [the tutorial](README.md). Attempt every problem before opening
> [the solutions](solutions.md).

Thirty-six problems, numbered continuously and grouped by difficulty. The theorem numbers refer to the
tutorial. Radian measures are exact; decimal answers are rounded to two places.

## Beginner

**1.** A circle has radius $7$. Find its circumference and its area, exactly and to two decimal places.

**2.** A circle has diameter $20$. Find its radius, circumference and area.

**3.** A circle has circumference $36\pi$. Find its radius and its area.

**4.** A circle has area $64\pi$. Find its radius and its circumference.

**5.** Convert to radians: (a) $30^\circ$; (b) $45^\circ$; (c) $120^\circ$; (d) $270^\circ$. Convert to degrees:
(e) $\tfrac{\pi}{6}$; (f) $\tfrac{3\pi}{4}$; (g) $\tfrac{5\pi}{3}$.

**6.** A sector of a circle of radius $6$ has angle $60^\circ$. Find its arc length and its area.

**7.** A sector of a circle of radius $9$ has angle $120^\circ$. Find its arc length and its area.

**8.** A circle has radius $13$ and a chord of length $24$. How far is the chord from the centre?

**9.** A chord lies at distance $6$ from the centre of a circle of radius $10$. Find its length.

**10.** A central angle measures $100^\circ$. (a) Find the inscribed angle that subtends the same arc, with its
vertex on the major arc. (b) Find the angle subtended by the other arc, with its vertex on the minor arc.

**11.** Three angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are $100^\circ$, $65^\circ$ and $80^\circ$, taken in order. Find
the fourth angle and verify both pairs.

**12.** From a point $P$ with $OP = 13$, a tangent is drawn to a circle of radius $5$. Find the length of the
tangent segment.

## Intermediate

**13.** Two tangents from $P$ touch a circle at $A$ and $B$, and $\angle APB = 90^\circ$ with $PA = 9$. Find
the radius and $OP$.

**14.** Two chords meet at an interior point $P$; one is cut into pieces of lengths $6$ and $8$, the other into
pieces of lengths $3$ and $y$. Find $y$.

**15.** Two chords meet at an interior point $P$, cutting each other into pieces of lengths $x$ and $x+4$, and
$5$ and $9$. Find $x$.

**16.** From an exterior point $P$, a secant meets the circle at $A$ and $B$ with $PA = 5$, $PB = 20$; another
secant meets it at $C$ and $D$ with $PC = 4$. Find $PD$.

**17.** From an exterior point $P$, a tangent $PT$ has length $12$, and a secant meets the circle at $A$ and $B$
with $PA = 9$. Find $PB$ and $AB$.

**18.** (a) Write the equation of the circle with centre $(3,-4)$ and radius $5$. (b) Find the centre and
radius of the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y - 11 = 0$.

**19.** Decide whether each point lies on, inside or outside the circle $x^2+y^2 = 61$: (a) $(5,6)$;
(b) $(-2,7)$; (c) $(0,0)$.

**20.** $AB$ is a diameter of a circle and $C$ lies on the circle with $\angle ABC = 35^\circ$. Find
$\angle BAC$ and $\angle ACB$.

**21.** A parallelogram is inscribed in a circle. Prove that it is a rectangle.

**22.** A circle of radius $10$ has two parallel chords of lengths $12$ and $16$ on the same side of the
centre. Find the distance between them.

## Advanced

**23.** A circle of radius $13$ has two parallel chords of lengths $10$ and $24$. Find the distance between
them in both possible configurations.

**24.** A chord of length $16$ lies at distance $6$ from the centre of a circle. Find the radius, and the
length of the chord at distance $8$ from the centre.

**25.** A sector has radius $10$ and arc length $15$. Find its central angle in radians and its area.

**26.** Two chords meet at an interior point $P$, with $PA = 3$ and $PB = 8$. If $P$ is the midpoint of the
other chord $CD$, find the length $CD$.

**27.** Prove that two equal chords of a circle cut off equal minor arcs and equal major arcs.

**28.** Two chords $AB$ and $CD$ meet at an interior point $P$. Prove that

$$
\angle APB = \tfrac12\left(m(\text{arc } AD) + m(\text{arc } BC)\right).
$$

**29.** A cyclic quadrilateral has one pair of opposite sides parallel. Prove that it is an isosceles
trapezium.

**30.** A tangent at a point $P$ of a circle is parallel to a chord $AB$. Prove that $P$ is the midpoint of the
arc $AB$.

## Challenge

**31.** Prove that the inradius of a right triangle with legs $a$, $b$ and hypotenuse $c$ is
$r = \tfrac12(a+b-c)$.

**32.** *(Ptolemy.)* Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Prove that
$AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot AD$. *Hint:* take $E$ on $AC$ with $\angle ABE = \angle CBD$ and use two
pairs of similar triangles.

**33.** Prove that the circumradius of a triangle with sides $a$, $b$, $c$ and area $K$ is
$R = \dfrac{abc}{4K}$.

**34.** A chord of the outer of two concentric circles is tangent to the inner circle and has length $d$.
Prove that the area of the annulus between the circles is $\pi\left(\tfrac{d}{2}\right)^2$, and evaluate it
when $d = 8$.

**35.** $AB$ is a diameter of a circle, $C$ is a point of the circle, and $CD$ is perpendicular to $AB$ with
$D$ on $AB$. Prove that $CD^2 = AD \cdot DB$ and that $AC^2 = AD \cdot AB$.

**36.** Two circles are internally tangent at $T$. Prove that the tangent to the larger circle at $T$ is also
tangent to the smaller circle, and that the two centres and $T$ are collinear.
