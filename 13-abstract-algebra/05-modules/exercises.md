# Modules — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Attempt every problem on paper before consulting [solutions.md](solutions.md). You may use any result
proved in the [tutorial](README.md). Before using a theorem, state its hypotheses; in particular, note
whether the ring is a PID and whether the module is finitely generated. For every computation with a
presentation matrix, state the matrix and its Smith normal form.

## Beginner

**1.** Use the submodule criterion to decide whether each subset is a submodule: (a) $2\mathbb{Z}\subseteq\mathbb{Z}$;
(b) the set of odd integers in $\mathbb{Z}$; (c) $\{(a,b)\in\mathbb{Z}^2 : a+b \text{ is even}\}\subseteq\mathbb{Z}^2$;
(d) $\{0,3,6,9\}\subseteq\mathbb{Z}/12$.

**2.** Show that the ideals of a commutative ring $R$ are exactly the submodules of the regular module $R$.
List all submodules of $\mathbb{Z}/20$ and draw their inclusion diagram.

**3.** Show that $\mathbb{Z}/n$ is a cyclic $\mathbb{Z}$-module generated by $\bar 1$. In $M=\mathbb{Z}/12$,
compute $\operatorname{Ann}(\bar 4)$, $\operatorname{Ann}(\bar 6)$ and $\operatorname{Ann}(M)$.

**4.** Show that a nonzero free module over an integral domain is torsion-free. Deduce that
$\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ is not a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module.

**5.** (a) Show that $R[x]$ is a free $R$-module with basis $\{1,x,x^2,\dots\}$. (b) Show that the ideal
$(x)\subseteq R[x]$ is free of rank $1$. (c) Show that $R[x]/(x)\cong R$.

**6.** Show that $\mathbb{Z}/2\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2$ is not cyclic, while $\mathbb{Z}/2\oplus\mathbb{Z}/3$
is cyclic, and exhibit a generator.

**7.** For $M=\mathbb{Z}/6$, list all submodules, draw the lattice, and determine which submodules are
direct summands.

**8.** Compute $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}/n)$ and
$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/n,\mathbb{Z})$.

**9.** Let $f:\mathbb{Z}/4\to\mathbb{Z}/8$ be defined by $f(\bar 1)=\bar 2$ (and extended $\mathbb{Z}$-linearly).
Check that $f$ is well defined, compute $\ker f$ and $\operatorname{im}f$, and verify the first
isomorphism theorem.

**10.** Show that $f:\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}$, $f(n)=2n$, is $\mathbb{Z}$-linear and injective, identify its
cokernel, and explain which vector-space theorem fails.

**11.** For $M=\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2$, compute the torsion submodule, the annihilator
$\operatorname{Ann}(M)$, and the quotient $M/M_{\mathrm{tor}}$.

**12.** Decide whether each module is free, giving a proof or a counterexample: (a) $\mathbb{Z}/6$ over
$\mathbb{Z}$; (b) $\mathbb{Z}/6$ over $\mathbb{Z}/6$; (c) $\mathbb{Z}$ over $\mathbb{Z}/6$; (d) $\mathbb{Q}$ over
$\mathbb{Z}$; (e) $\mathbb{Z}$ over $\mathbb{Z}$.

## Intermediate

**13.** Classify the abelian groups of order $200=2^3\cdot5^2$: state how many there are and list them in
elementary-divisor and invariant-factor form.

**14.** (a) How many abelian groups of order $10^6$ are there? (b) Show that
$\mathbb{Z}/4\oplus\mathbb{Z}/9\cong\mathbb{Z}/36$ and that $\mathbb{Z}/2\oplus\mathbb{Z}/18$ is not
isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/6\oplus\mathbb{Z}/6$.

**15.** Compute the invariant factors of $\mathbb{Z}^3/A\mathbb{Z}^3$ for
$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&1\\0&3&1\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix}$, and identify the module.

**16.** Prove that $\mathbb{Z}/a\oplus\mathbb{Z}/b\cong\mathbb{Z}/\gcd(a,b)\oplus\mathbb{Z}/\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$
for positive integers $a,b$.

**17.** Compute: (a) $\mathbb{Z}/8\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}/12$; (b) $\mathbb{Z}/6\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}/4$;
(c) $\mathbb{Z}/2\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q}$; (d) $\mathbb{Z}/5\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}/5$.

**18.** Show that $(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}/n=0$ for every $n\ge1$.

**19.** Compute $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^2-1)\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{R}[x]/(x-1)$ and
$\mathbb{C}\otimes_{\mathbb{R}}\mathbb{R}[x]$.

**20.** Show that $\mathbb{Z}[x]\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z}[y]\cong\mathbb{Z}[x,y]$ by exhibiting a
bilinear map and its inverse.

**21.** Which of these short exact sequences split? Justify each. (a)
$0\to\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{n\mapsto(n,0)}\mathbb{Z}^2\xrightarrow{(a,b)\mapsto b}\mathbb{Z}\to0$;
(b) $0\to\mathbb{Z}/2\xrightarrow{\bar1\mapsto\bar2}\mathbb{Z}/4\to\mathbb{Z}/2\to0$;
(c) $0\to\mathbb{Z}/2\to\mathbb{Z}/6\to\mathbb{Z}/3\to0$.

**22.** Show that $\mathbb{Z}/n$ is not a projective $\mathbb{Z}$-module for $n\ge2$, by showing that the
identity of $\mathbb{Z}/n$ cannot be lifted along $\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}/n$.

**23.** Compute $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/6,\mathbb{Z}/10)$ and
$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/12,\mathbb{Z}/18)$: give the number of elements and describe
them explicitly.

## Advanced

**24.** Prove the second and third isomorphism theorems for $R$-modules, stating precisely which submodules
are involved.

**25.** Suppose that every short exact sequence of $R$-modules splits. Prove that every $R$-module is both
projective and injective.

**26.** Prove that $P$ is projective if and only if $\operatorname{Hom}_R(P,-)$ is exact.

**27.** Prove that a finitely generated module is projective if and only if it is a direct summand of a
finitely generated free module.

**28.** Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a local ring and $M$ a finitely generated $R$-module. Prove carefully
that every minimal generating set of $M$ has $\dim_{R/\mathfrak m}(M/\mathfrak mM)$ elements.

**29.** Let $R$ be a PID with fraction field $K$, and $M$ a finitely generated $R$-module. Prove that $M$
is a torsion module if and only if $M\otimes_RK=0$.

**30.** (a) Show that $\mathbb{Z}$ is not an injective $\mathbb{Z}$-module. (b) Show that
$\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ is divisible and hence injective.

**31.** Prove that a short exact sequence $0\to A\to B\xrightarrow{g}C\to0$ with $C$ free splits.

## Challenge

**32.** Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a local ring and $P$ a finitely generated projective $R$-module. Prove
that $P$ is free. (Use Nakayama's lemma.)

**33.** Prove that the ideal $(x,y)\subseteq k[x,y]$ is not a projective $k[x,y]$-module.

**34.** Prove that a commutative ring $R$ is a field if and only if every $R$-module is free.

**35.** Prove that $\mathbb{Q}$ is not a projective $\mathbb{Z}$-module.

**36.** Prove that two finitely generated abelian groups $G,H$ are isomorphic if and only if
$G/nG\cong H/nH$ for every $n\ge1$.
