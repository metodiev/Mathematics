# Exercises: Ratios, Rates and Proportions

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Beginner → Challenge · Full solutions in [solutions.md](solutions.md)

Show your working. Keep the units attached at every step, and label both sides of every proportion with the quantities they represent. Problems marked with a proof require a complete argument, not an example.

## Beginner (1–12)

**1.** Write the ratio $15:25$ in simplest form.

**2.** A class contains $18$ boys and $24$ girls. Find (a) the ratio of boys to girls, (b) the ratio of boys to all students, and (c) the fraction of the class that is girls.

**3.** Simplify the ratio $3.6:0.48$.

**4.** Simplify the ratio $50\text{ min}:2\text{ h}$.

**5.** A train travels $275$ km in $5$ hours. Find its average speed in kilometres per hour.

**6.** Six kilograms of potatoes cost $\$15.60$. Find the unit price in dollars per kilogram.

**7.** Solve the proportion $\dfrac{x}{5}=\dfrac{12}{20}$.

**8.** Solve the proportion $4:7=20:x$.

**9.** A recipe uses flour and sugar in the ratio $5:2$. If $15$ cups of flour are used, how many cups of sugar are needed?

**10.** The quantity $y$ varies directly with $x$, with constant of proportionality $k=4.5$. Find $y$ when $x=8$, and find $x$ when $y=27$.

**11.** A map has scale $1:200000$. Two villages are $3.5$ cm apart on the map. Find the actual distance in kilometres.

**12.** Convert $72$ km/h to metres per second.

## Intermediate (13–22)

**13.** Divide $\$720$ among three people in the ratio $3:4:5$.

**14.** Two numbers are in the ratio $5:8$, and their difference is $27$. Find both numbers.

**15.** Increase $60$ in the ratio $7:5$.

**16.** A $24$ L mixture contains juice and water in the ratio $3:5$. How much water must be added so that the ratio becomes $3:7$?

**17.** A car covers $150$ km in $2.5$ hours. At the same average speed, how far does it travel in $4.2$ hours?

**18.** The quantity $y$ varies inversely with $x$, and $y=12$ when $x=7$. Find $y$ when $x=21$.

**19.** A shop sells $3$ kg of rice for $\$11.40$ and $5$ kg for $\$18.50$. Which offer has the lower unit price, and by how much per kilogram?

**20.** A rectangular room measures $4.8$ m by $3.6$ m. A scale drawing uses the scale $1:80$. Find the dimensions of the room on the drawing, in centimetres.

**21.** A triangle has sides $9$ cm, $12$ cm, and $15$ cm. A similar triangle has shortest side $15$ cm. Find its other two sides and its perimeter.

**22.** Convert the density $0.92$ g/cm$^3$ to kg/m$^3$.

## Advanced (23–31)

**23.** Prove that if $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{c}{d}$ with $b,d\neq 0$, then

$$
\frac{a+b}{b}=\frac{c+d}{d}.
$$

Illustrate the identity with $a=3$, $b=4$, $c=9$, $d=12$.

**24.** A car travels $45$ km on $4$ L of fuel.

(a) How many litres does it need for $180$ km at the same rate?
(b) If fuel costs $\$1.65$ per litre, what is the fuel cost of the $180$ km journey?
(c) How far can the car travel on $10$ L?

**25.** Twelve workers can build a wall in $15$ days. After $5$ days, $3$ workers leave. Assuming all workers work at the same constant rate, how many more days are needed to finish the wall?

**26.** A map has scale $1:50000$. A rectangular plot measures $4$ cm by $2.5$ cm on the map. Find its actual area in square kilometres.

**27.** How many litres of $10\%$ saline solution and of $50\%$ saline solution must be mixed to obtain $40$ L of $20\%$ saline solution?

**28.** (a) Prove that scaling a rectangle by a factor $k>0$ multiplies its area by $k^2$. (b) Explain why the same conclusion holds for any two similar plane figures.

**29.** A photograph measures $10$ cm by $15$ cm. It is enlarged, keeping the same shape, so that the longer side becomes $24$ cm. Find the new shorter side and the factor by which the area increases.

**30.** (a) Convert $1$ kg/m$^3$ to g/cm$^3$. (b) Convert $60$ miles per hour to kilometres per hour and to metres per second, using $1$ mile $=1.609344$ km.

**31.** A quantity $A$ is directly proportional to a quantity $B$. If $B$ increases by $10\%$, by what percentage does $A$ increase? Answer the same question if $A$ is inversely proportional to $B$. Justify both answers.

## Challenge (32–36)

**32.** Suppose

$$
\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}=\frac{e}{f},
$$

where $b,d,f\neq 0$ and $b+d+f\neq 0$. Prove that the common value of the three fractions is also equal to

$$
\frac{a+c+e}{b+d+f}.
$$

**33.** Two candles have the same height. The first burns completely in $4$ hours, the second in $6$ hours, each at a constant rate. They are lit at the same moment. After how many hours is one candle exactly twice as tall as the other? Which candle is taller at that moment?

**34.** A rectangular garden is enlarged so that its perimeter increases by $20\%$ while its shape remains similar. By what percentage does its area increase?

**35.** A $20$ L mixture contains milk and water in the ratio $3:1$. Some of the mixture is removed and replaced by the same volume of water, after which the ratio of milk to water is $3:2$. How many litres were removed?

**36.** Three cats catch three mice in three minutes, all working at the same constant rate. How many cats are needed to catch $100$ mice in $100$ minutes?
