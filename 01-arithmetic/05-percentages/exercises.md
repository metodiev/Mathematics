# Percentages — Exercises

> [Percentages](README.md) · 36 problems · Attempt all problems before reading [solutions.md](solutions.md).

Identify the base of every percentage statement before calculating. Unless a problem says
otherwise, give exact answers where possible and round money to the nearest cent.

## Beginner

**1.** A phone battery is $45\%$ charged. Write $45\%$ as a fraction in lowest terms and as a
decimal.

**2.** A recipe uses $\tfrac{5}{8}$ of a kilogram of flour. Write this amount as a decimal and as a
percentage.

**3.** A sales tax rate is $7\%$. Write $7\%$ as a decimal and as a fraction in lowest terms.

**4.** A shirt originally costs $\$84$ and is discounted by $25\%$. What is the discount, and what
is the sale price?

**5.** A restaurant bill is $\$52$. An $18\%$ tip is added. How much is the tip, and what is the
total paid?

**6.** In a survey, $20$ of $80$ respondents prefer tea. What percentage is that?

**7.** A class has $18$ students who wear glasses, which is $30\%$ of the class. How many students
are in the class?

**8.** A train fare of $\$90$ increases by $20\%$. What is the new fare?

**9.** A jacket priced at $\$180$ is reduced by $15\%$. What is the sale price?

**10.** A solution contains $0.006$ grams of a trace mineral per litre. Write $0.006$ as a
percentage, and write $150\%$ as a decimal.

**11.** A student answers $42$ of $50$ questions correctly. What percentage is correct?

**12.** A worker's hourly wage is $\$18.50$ and rises by $4\%$. What is the new wage?

## Intermediate

**13.** A $\$45$ item rises in price by $8\%$. What is the new price?

**14.** After a $30\%$ discount, an item costs $\$84$. What was the original price?

**15.** A town's population grows by $4\%$ in one year and by $3\%$ in the next. What is the total
percentage growth over the two years?

**16.** A salary rises by $8\%$ and then falls by $8\%$. What is the net percentage change?

**17.** A shop offers successive discounts of $25\%$ and $12\%$. What single discount is
equivalent?

**18.** A shop buys an item for $\$80$, marks it up by $30\%$, then discounts the marked price by
$20\%$. Find the selling price and the profit as a percentage of the cost.

**19.** Sales tax is $7.5\%$. What is the total cost of a $\$180$ purchase?

**20.** An unemployment rate rises from $4.5\%$ to $5.4\%$. State the change in percentage points
and the relative percentage change.

**21.** $\$2{,}000$ is invested at $3.5\%$ simple interest for $4$ years. Find the interest and the
final amount.

**22.** $\$2{,}000$ is invested at $3.5\%$ compounded annually for $4$ years. Find the final amount
to the nearest cent.

**23.** A price is increased by $25\%$. What percentage decrease returns it to the original price?

**24.** A price including $20\%$ VAT is $\$186$. What is the price before tax?

## Advanced

**25.** A quantity increases by $p\%$ and then decreases by $p\%$. Derive the net percentage
change, and evaluate it for $p=15$.

**26.** After increases of $10\%$ and then $20\%$, a quantity is $132$. Find the original value.

**27.** A measured length is $12.4$ cm; the true length is $12.5$ cm. Compute the percent error.

**28.** $200$ mL of a $15\%$ saline solution is mixed with $50$ mL of pure water. Assuming the
densities are $1$ g/mL and the volumes add, what is the new mass percentage of salt?

**29.** Prove that for every integer $t>1$ and every real $r>0$,
$(1+r)^{t}>1+rt$. Explain what this inequality says about compound versus simple interest.

**30.** In a school, a group of $40$ students has a pass rate of $75\%$ and a group of $10$
students has a pass rate of $90\%$. What is the overall pass rate?

**31.** A bank quotes a nominal annual rate of $5\%$ compounded quarterly. Compute the effective
annual rate, correct to four decimal places as a percentage.

## Challenge

**32.** $A$ is $20\%$ of $B$, and $B$ is $30\%$ of $C$. What percentage of $C$ is $A$? Prove in
general that $p\%$ of $q\%$ of a whole is $\dfrac{pq}{100}\%$ of the whole.

**33.** A quantity is decreased by $p\%$. Derive the percentage increase $q\%$ that restores the
original value, and evaluate $q$ for $p=20$ and $p=50$.

**34.** At an annual compound rate of $8\%$, compare the rule of $72$ estimate of the doubling time
with the exact doubling time $\ln 2/\ln 1.08$. Explain why the rule of $72$ is only approximate.

**35.** $A$ is $20\%$ more than $B$. What percentage less is $B$ than $A$? Separately, $C$ is $20\%$
less than $D$. What percentage more is $D$ than $C$? Prove the general formulas and explain why the
two answers differ.

**36.** Store A offers $25\%$ off and then an additional $10\%$ off the reduced price. Store B
offers a single $35\%$ discount. Which store is cheaper, and by how many percentage points of the
original price? Explain why the two successive discounts of Store A do not equal $35\%$.
