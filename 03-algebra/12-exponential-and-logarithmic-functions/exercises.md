# Exponential and Logarithmic Functions — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 40 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Work each problem completely. For every logarithmic equation, write the domain conditions **before**
solving and check every candidate against them at the end. For applications, state the model, substitute
the data, and give units with the final answer. Give exact answers where they exist and decimals only
as approximations. Full solutions are in [solutions.md](solutions.md); do not open that file until you
have attempted the problems.

## Beginner

**1.** Evaluate without a calculator: $2^5$, $3^{-2}$, $16^{3/4}$, $27^{1/3}$ and
$\left(\tfrac14\right)^{-1/2}$.

**2.** Simplify, assuming all variables are positive: (a) $\dfrac{a^3a^{-5}}{a^{-1}}$;
(b) $\left(2x^2\right)^3x^{-4}$.

**3.** For $y=3^x$, state the domain, the range, the $y$-intercept, the horizontal asymptote and the
monotonicity, and say whether the graph ever meets the $x$-axis.

**4.** For $y=\left(\tfrac13\right)^x$, state the same five properties, and explain how its graph is
related to the graph of $y=3^x$.

**5.** Evaluate without a calculator: $\log_3 81$, $\log_2\dfrac18$, $\log_{16}4$, $\log_5 5$ and
$\ln 1$.

**6.** Rewrite each statement in the other form: (a) $2^7=128$; (b) $10^{-3}=0.001$;
(c) $\log_4 64=3$; (d) $\log_{1/2}8=-3$.

**7.** Simplify: $e^{\ln 5}$, $10^{\log_{10}7}$, $\ln e^3$, $\log_2 2^{9}$.

**8.** Write as a single logarithm: $\log_2 6+\log_2 4-\log_2 3$.

**9.** Expand completely: (a) $\log_a\left(x^2y^3\right)$; (b) $\log_a\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{y}$.

**10.** Use change of base to evaluate $\log_8 32$ exactly.

**11.** Solve: (a) $2^x=32$; (b) $3^x=\dfrac{1}{27}$; (c) $5^{2x}=125$.

**12.** $\$1000$ is invested at a nominal annual rate of $5\%$ compounded monthly for two years. Compute
the balance, to the nearest cent.

**13.** A radioactive substance has a half-life of $20$ years. What fraction of the original amount
remains after $60$ years?

## Intermediate

**14.** Simplify: (a) $27^{2/3}\cdot 9^{1/2}$; (b) $8^{-2/3}$.

**15.** Expand: $\ln\dfrac{x^2\sqrt{y}}{z^3}$, where $x,y,z>0$.

**16.** Write as a single logarithm: $3\log x-\tfrac12\log y+\log z$, where $x,y,z>0$.

**17.** Solve $5^{2x-1}=40$. Give the exact answer and a decimal approximation to four places.

**18.** Solve $9^x-4\cdot 3^x+3=0$.

**19.** Solve $e^{2x}-3e^x-4=0$. Give the exact answer and a decimal approximation.

**20.** Solve $\log_3(x+2)+\log_3 x=1$.

**21.** Solve $\log_2 x-\log_2(x-3)=2$.

**22.** Solve $\log(x+3)+\log x=1$, where $\log$ denotes the base-$10$ logarithm.

**23.** Solve the system
$$\begin{cases}2^x\cdot 4^y = 32,\\[2pt] \dfrac{3^x}{9^y}=\dfrac13 .\end{cases}$$

**24.** A drug has a half-life of $8$ days. How long does it take for only $15\%$ of the dose to remain?
Give the answer to the nearest tenth of a day.

**25.** A sample of bone retains $45\%$ of its original carbon-14. Estimate its age, using
$T_{1/2}=5730$ years. Give the answer to the nearest ten years.

**26.** (a) How many times greater is the amplitude of a magnitude $6.5$ earthquake than that of a
magnitude $4.5$ earthquake? (b) How many times greater is the energy released, if energy scales as
$A^{3/2}$?

**27.** (a) Two sounds have levels $65$ dB and $85$ dB. Find the ratio of their intensities.
(b) Find the sound level, in decibels, of an intensity of $10^{-5}$ W/m$^2$, given
$I_0=10^{-12}$ W/m$^2$.

## Advanced

**28.** Prove the power law $\log_a(x^r)=r\log_a x$ for $x>0$, first for positive integers $r$, then
for $r=1/n$, then for arbitrary rational $r$.

**29.** Prove the change-of-base formula $\log_b x=\dfrac{\log_a x}{\log_a b}$, and deduce
$\log_a b=\dfrac{1}{\log_b a}$.

**30.** Prove that the sequence $a_n=\left(1+\tfrac1n\right)^n$ is bounded above by $3$, and explain why
this together with monotonicity guarantees that $\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n$ exists.

**31.** Solve $\log_2 x+\log_4 x+\log_8 x=11$.

**32.** Solve $x^{\log_2 x}=8$. Give both exact solutions and their decimal approximations.

**33.** Prove: (a) $\log_a b\cdot\log_b c\cdot\log_c a=1$ for $a,b,c>0$, all different from $1$;
(b) $\log_{a^n}\left(b^m\right)=\dfrac mn\log_a b$ for $a,b>0$, $a\ne1$, $n\ne0$.

**34.** Two bone samples from the same site retain $30\%$ and $90\%$ of their carbon-14. Estimate both
ages ($T_{1/2}=5730$ years), and explain why the estimate based on $90\%$ is far less reliable than the
one based on $30\%$.

**35.** Let $f$ be continuous and satisfy $f(x+y)=f(x)f(y)$ for all real $x,y$, with $f(1)=3$. Find
$f(0)$, $f(2)$, $f(-1)$, $f\!\left(\tfrac12\right)$ and $f\!\left(\tfrac32\right)$, and identify $f$.

**36.** For $y=-2\cdot 3^{x-1}+4$: describe the transformations from $y=3^x$, state the domain, the
range, the horizontal asymptote, the $y$-intercept and the $x$-intercept, and say whether the function
is increasing or decreasing.

**37.** For $y=\ln(x-2)+1$: state the domain, the range, the vertical asymptote and the $x$-intercept,
find the inverse function, and state its domain and range.

## Challenge

**38.** The logistic model is $P(t)=\dfrac{K}{1+Ae^{-rt}}$ with $K,r>0$ and $A>0$.
(a) Show that $P(0)=\dfrac{K}{1+A}$, and conclude that a given initial value $P_0 \in (0,K)$ corresponds
to $A=\dfrac{K-P_0}{P_0}$. (b) Show that $P(t)$ is strictly increasing. (c) Show that $P(t)\to K$ as
$t\to\infty$. (d) Solve $P(t)=\tfrac K2$ for $t$, and explain why this is the time of fastest growth.

**39.** Solve $\log_2\left(3^x-1\right)=x$, and prove that the solution you found is the only one.

**40.** Prove that $\log_2 3$ is irrational.
