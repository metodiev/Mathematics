# Rational Expressions — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Work each problem completely: state the domain before simplifying, show the factoring, and check at
least one value by substitution. For equations, say explicitly whether a rejected candidate is
excluded by the domain or by the physical situation. Full solutions are in [solutions.md](solutions.md);
do not open that file until you have attempted the problems.

## Beginner

**1.** State the domain of $\dfrac{5x}{x-7}$.

**2.** State the domain of $\dfrac{x+2}{x^2-9}$.

**3.** State the domain of $\dfrac{3x-1}{x^2+4}$ and explain why no value is excluded.

**4.** Simplify $\dfrac{12x^3y}{18xy^4}$ and state the domain.

**5.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^2-25}{x-5}$, stating the excluded value. What number does the simplified
expression give at $x=5$, and why is that not the value of the original expression there?

**6.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^2+7x+12}{x^2-9}$ and state the domain.

**7.** Multiply $\dfrac{3}{x}\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{9}$ and state the domain.

**8.** Divide $\dfrac{x}{4}\div\dfrac{x^2}{8}$ and state the domain.

**9.** Add $\dfrac{2}{x}+\dfrac{3}{x}$ and state the domain.

**10.** Subtract $\dfrac{5}{x-1}-\dfrac{2}{x-1}$ and state the domain.

**11.** Solve the proportion $\dfrac{x}{3}=\dfrac{4}{5}$.

**12.** Solve $\dfrac{2}{x}=\dfrac{5}{x+3}$.

## Intermediate

**13.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^2-x-6}{x^2-4x+3}$ and state the domain.

**14.** Multiply $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x^2+2x}\cdot\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^2+x-2}$ and state the domain.

**15.** Divide $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x^2+4x+4}\div\dfrac{x-3}{x+2}$ and state the domain.

**16.** Simplify the complex fraction $\dfrac{\frac1x+\frac1y}{\frac1x-\frac1y}$ and state the domain.

**17.** Simplify $\dfrac{1-\frac{9}{x^2}}{1+\frac{3}{x}}$ and state the domain.

**18.** Add $\dfrac{3}{x-2}+\dfrac{2}{x+3}$.

**19.** Subtract $\dfrac{x}{x^2-9}-\dfrac{1}{x-3}$.

**20.** Solve $\dfrac{x}{x-3}-\dfrac{9}{x^2-3x}=\dfrac{1}{x}$ and identify any extraneous candidate.

**21.** Solve the proportion $\dfrac{3x-2}{x+5}=\dfrac{2}{x}$.

**22.** Working alone, Ana takes $4$ hours longer than Ben to assemble a machine. Working together
they take $3$ hours. How long does each take alone?

## Advanced

**23.** Simplify $\dfrac{\frac{1}{x-2}-\frac{2}{x^2-4}}{\frac{1}{x+2}}$ and state the domain.

**24.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^{-1}-y^{-1}}{x^{-1}+y^{-1}}$ and state the domain.

**25.** Solve $\dfrac{6}{x^2-1}=\dfrac{1}{x-1}-\dfrac{2}{x+1}$.

**26.** Solve $\dfrac{3}{x-1}-\dfrac{2}{x+2}=\dfrac{5}{x^2+x-2}$.

**27.** The quantity $y$ varies inversely as the square of $x$. When $x=2$, $y=3$.
(a) Find the constant of variation. (b) Find $y$ when $x=6$. (c) Find every $x$ for which
$y=\frac34$.

**28.** A workshop has fixed daily costs of $\$8000$ and a variable cost of $\$6$ per unit. The
average cost per unit at a production level of $x\ge1$ units is $\bar C(x)=\dfrac{8000+6x}{x}$.
(a) Compute $\bar C(100)$ and $\bar C(1000)$. (b) State both asymptotes and interpret them.
(c) Find the production levels at which the average cost is at most $\$8$.

**29.** Analyse $R(x)=\dfrac{3x^2-3}{x^2+2x-3}$: give the domain, any holes, the vertical asymptote,
the horizontal asymptote and the zeros.

**30.** Prove that for real numbers $a,b,c,d$ with $b\ne0$ and $d\ne0$,
$$\frac ab=\frac cd\iff ad=bc.$$
State exactly where each hypothesis is used.

## Challenge

**31.** Find every value of $k$ for which the equation
$$\frac{x}{x-2}=\frac{k}{x-2}+3$$
has no solution. Justify that all other values of $k$ give exactly one solution.

**32.** Analyse $R(x)=\dfrac{x^3-x}{x^2+x-2}$: give the domain, any holes, the vertical asymptote,
the zeros, and the slant asymptote obtained by polynomial division.

**33.** Two pipes together fill a tank in $2$ hours $24$ minutes. One pipe alone takes $2$ hours
longer than the other. Find the time each pipe needs alone.

**34.** A tank holds $60$ litres of a $30\%$ acid solution. How much pure acid must be added to raise
the concentration to $50\%$? Solve with a rational equation and check the concentration afterwards.

**35.** Prove the following. Let $M$ be a rational expression, $a\in\operatorname{dom}M$, and suppose
$M(a)\ne0$. Then for expressions $A,B$ defined at $a$,
$$A(a)=B(a)\iff A(a)M(a)=B(a)M(a).$$
Deduce that every solution of $A(x)M(x)=B(x)M(x)$ either solves $A(x)=B(x)$ or satisfies $M(x)=0$, and
explain why this makes checking candidates mandatory when clearing denominators.

**36.** Solve $\dfrac{1}{x-1}+\dfrac{1}{x-2}+\dfrac{1}{x-3}=0$ and state the solution set exactly (no
decimal approximations).
