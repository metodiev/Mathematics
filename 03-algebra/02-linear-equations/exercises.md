# Linear Equations — Exercises

> Tutorial: [README.md](README.md) · Solutions: [solutions.md](solutions.md)

Solve each equation by justified equivalence steps. For equations in one variable, state whether the equation is conditional, an identity, or a contradiction. For absolute-value equations, check every candidate in the original equation. For word problems, define the variable with its unit, write the equation from a stated principle, and check the answer against the wording.

## Beginner

**1.** Solve $x+7=12$.

**2.** Solve $3x=-15$.

**3.** Solve $2x-5=9$.

**4.** Solve $\dfrac{x}{4}+3=7$.

**5.** Solve $5-x=2$.

**6.** Solve $4x+3=2x+11$.

**7.** Solve $3(x-2)=12$.

**8.** Solve $2(x+1)=2x+2$ and classify the equation.

**9.** Solve $3x-4=3x+7$ and classify the equation.

**10.** Solve $|x|=6$.

**11.** Solve $|x-4|=3$.

**12.** Solve $3x+y=10$ for $y$.

## Intermediate

**13.** Solve $5x-3=2x+12$.

**14.** Solve $7-2(x-3)=4x+1$.

**15.** Solve $3(2x+1)-4=2(3x-5)+9$ and classify the equation.

**16.** Solve $\dfrac12x+\dfrac13=\dfrac34x-1$.

**17.** Solve $\dfrac{2x-1}{3}=\dfrac{x+4}{2}$.

**18.** Solve $0.5x-1.2=0.2x+0.9$.

**19.** Solve $0.03x+0.02=0.05x-0.04$.

**20.** Solve $|2x+1|=11$.

**21.** Solve $|5-3x|=4$.

**22.** Solve $|x|+3=1$.

**23.** Solve $\dfrac{a+b}{2}=c$ for $b$.

**24.** Solve $S=P(1+rt)$ for $r$, stating the necessary conditions.

## Advanced

**25.** Solve $\dfrac34-\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac{2x+1}{6}$.

**26.** Solve $\dfrac{x-3}{4}-\dfrac{2x+1}{6}=\dfrac12$.

**27.** Solve $0.02(3x-1)=0.05(x+2)$.

**28.** Solve $2-\dfrac{x+3}{5}=\dfrac{2x-1}{3}$.

**29.** Solve $|3x-6|=0$.

**30.** Solve $|2x-3|=|x+1|$.

**31.** Solve $a(x+b)=c(x-d)$ for $x$, where $a\ne c$.

**32.** A mother is three times as old as her daughter. In $12$ years she will be twice as old. Find their present ages.

**33.** Pipe A fills a tank in $9$ hours and pipe B fills it in $18$ hours. How long do they take working together?

**34.** Two trains leave the same station at the same time in opposite directions, one at $60$ km/h and the other at $80$ km/h. After how many hours are they $350$ km apart?

**35.** Ten litres of a $30\%$ acid solution are mixed with $20$ litres of a $60\%$ acid solution. What is the concentration of the resulting mixture?

**36.** How many litres of pure water must be added to $12$ litres of a $40\%$ salt solution to obtain a $30\%$ solution?

## Challenge

**37.** Solve $2(x-1)=3(x-1)$. Then explain what goes wrong if one begins by dividing both sides by $x-1$.

**38.** Solve $|2x-1|=3x+2$, checking every candidate in the original equation.

**39.** Solve $|x-a|=2a$ for $x$ in terms of $a$, assuming $a>0$. What happens when $a=0$? When $a<0$?

**40.** Find all real values of $k$ for which $|3x-6|=k$ has exactly one solution.

**41.** Prove the trichotomy for $ax+b=cx+d$: if $a\ne c$ there is exactly one solution; if $a=c$ and $b=d$ every real number is a solution; if $a=c$ and $b\ne d$ there is no solution.

**42.** Explain why squaring both sides of an equation can introduce extraneous solutions. Illustrate with the equations $x=3$ and $x^2=9$.

**43.** A cyclist leaves a town at 9:00 riding at $15$ km/h. A car leaves the same town at 10:00 travelling along the same route at $45$ km/h. When does the car catch the cyclist, and how far from the town does this happen?

**44.** Working together, A and B complete a job in $6$ days. A alone takes $10$ days. How long does B alone take?

**45.** A father is $5$ years more than twice his son's age. Eight years ago he was three times as old as his son. Find their present ages.
