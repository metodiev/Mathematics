# Intermediate Algebra — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Show every significant step. For rational expressions, state the domain restrictions. For radical
equations, check every candidate in the original equation. Full solutions are in
[solutions.md](solutions.md); attempt the problems first.

## Beginner

**1.** Evaluate $3^{-2}+2^{-1}$.

**2.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^4y^{-2}}{x^2y^3}$, where $x,y\neq0$.

**3.** Write $\sqrt{50}+\sqrt{18}-\sqrt8$ in simplest radical form.

**4.** Simplify $\sqrt[3]{54}-\sqrt[3]{16}$.

**5.** Rationalise the denominator: $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt3}$.

**6.** Rationalise the denominator: $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5+1}$.

**7.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x^2+x-12}$ and state the domain.

**8.** Multiply and simplify: $\dfrac{3}{x}\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{9}$, where $x\neq0$.

**9.** Divide and simplify: $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+2}\div\dfrac{x-1}{x^2-4}$. State the domain.

**10.** Use synthetic division to divide $2x^3+x^2-5x+2$ by $x+2$. State the quotient and remainder.

**11.** Find the remainder when $x^3+2x^2-5x+1$ is divided by $x+2$.

**12.** Expand $(2x-1)^3$.

## Intermediate

**13.** Simplify $\dfrac{(8x^6)^{2/3}}{(4x^4)^{1/2}}$, where $x>0$.

**14.** Simplify $\sqrt[4]{16x^4y^8}$ for all real $x,y$.

**15.** Rationalise the denominator: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{2}-1}$.

**16.** Add and simplify: $\dfrac{1}{x-2}+\dfrac{3}{x+2}$. State the domain.

**17.** Simplify the complex fraction $\dfrac{1+\frac1x}{1-\frac1x}$, where $x\neq0,1$.

**18.** Simplify the complex fraction $\dfrac{\frac{x}{x-1}-1}{\frac{1}{x-1}}$, where $x\neq1$.

**19.** Solve $\sqrt{3x+1}=x-1$.

**20.** Solve $\sqrt{x+5}=x-1$.

**21.** Solve $\sqrt[3]{2x-1}=-3$.

**22.** Divide $3x^3-5x^2+2x-7$ by $x-2$. State the quotient and remainder.

**23.** Given that $x=2$ is a root, factor $x^3+2x^2-5x-6$ completely.

**24.** Factor $3x^3-2x^2-12x+8$ by grouping.

## Advanced

**25.** Expand $(2x-3)^4$.

**26.** Find the coefficient of $x^5$ in the expansion of $(2x-1)^8$.

**27.** Solve $x^{2/3}+x^{1/3}-2=0$.

**28.** Solve $\sqrt{x+4}-\sqrt{x-1}=1$.

**29.** Simplify $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^3-8}\div\dfrac{x+2}{x^2+2x+4}$ and state the domain.

**30.** Find the real number $k$ such that $x-3$ is a factor of $2x^3-5x^2+kx-3$.

**31.** Prove that $\sqrt2$ is irrational.

## Challenge

**32.** Simplify $\sqrt{3+2\sqrt2}$ and $\sqrt{3-2\sqrt2}$ without a calculator. Justify why the
principal square roots have the signs you give.

**33.** Solve $x+\sqrt{x-1}=3$.

**34.** Solve $2x^{2/3}+x^{1/3}-1=0$.

**35.** Prove that $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk=2^n$ in two ways: a combinatorial argument
and an algebraic substitution in the binomial theorem.

**36.** Factor $x^4+4$ over the real numbers.
