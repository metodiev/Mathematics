# Quadratic Equations — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Write each equation in standard form, state the method you are using and why, show every algebraic
step, and **check every root by substitution**. Give exact answers (surds and fractions) unless a
problem asks for a decimal approximation. When an equation has no real solution, say so explicitly.
Full solutions are in [solutions.md](solutions.md); do not open that file until you have attempted the
problems.

## Beginner

**1.** Solve by square roots: $x^2 - 16 = 0$.

**2.** Solve: $5x^2 = 45$.

**3.** Solve by factoring: $x^2 + 6x + 8 = 0$.

**4.** Solve by factoring: $x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0$.

**5.** Solve: $3x^2 - 12x = 0$.

**6.** Solve: $(x - 5)^2 = 9$.

**7.** Solve: $x^2 + 2x + 1 = 0$.

**8.** Solve by the quadratic formula: $x^2 - 4x - 12 = 0$.

**9.** Solve by factoring: $2x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0$.

**10.** Compute the discriminant of $2x^2 - 3x + 4$ and state, with a reason, how many real solutions
the equation $2x^2 - 3x + 4 = 0$ has.

**11.** Write $y = x^2 - 8x + 11$ in vertex form and state its vertex.

**12.** For $y = 3(x+2)^2 - 12$, state the vertex, the axis of symmetry, and whether the parabola opens
upward or downward. Then find the $x$-intercepts.

## Intermediate

**13.** Solve by completing the square: $x^2 + 10x + 7 = 0$.

**14.** Solve by completing the square: $2x^2 - 8x + 3 = 0$.

**15.** Solve by the quadratic formula: $3x^2 - 7x + 2 = 0$.

**16.** Solve: $x^2 = 4x + 5$.

**17.** Find every value of $k$ for which $x^2 + kx + 25 = 0$ has exactly one real solution.

**18.** Show that $x^2 - 6x + 11 = 0$ has no real solutions, and give the complex solutions.

**19.** A rectangle is $4\ \mathrm{cm}$ longer than it is wide and has area $96\ \mathrm{cm^2}$. Find
its dimensions.

**20.** Solve: $\dfrac{x^2}{3} - \dfrac{x}{2} = 2$.

**21.** Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the roots of $3x^2 - 6x + 2 = 0$. Without solving, compute
$\alpha + \beta$, $\alpha\beta$, $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta}$ and $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$.

**22.** Find a quadratic equation with integer coefficients whose roots are $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $-4$.

**23.** Solve: $x^4 - 13x^2 + 36 = 0$.

**24.** Solve: $2(x - 1)^2 = 10$.

## Advanced

**25.** Solve over $\mathbb{C}$: $x^2 + 4x + 13 = 0$.

**26.** Solve $\sqrt{2x + 5} = x + 1$ and explain why one of the values obtained by squaring is not a
solution.

**27.** Solve the inequality $x^2 - 3x - 10 \leq 0$, giving the solution set in interval notation.

**28.** Solve the inequality $3x^2 + 2x - 8 > 0$.

**29.** For $y = -x^2 + 6x - 5$, write the parabola in vertex form, state the vertex, the axis, the
direction of opening, and both intercepts with the axes. Then describe the graph.

**30.** A ball is thrown vertically upward from ground level with initial speed $24.5\ \mathrm{m/s}$.
Its height in metres after $t$ seconds is $h(t) = -4.9t^2 + 24.5t$. Find the maximum height, the time
at which it occurs, and the time when the ball returns to the ground.

**31.** A shop sells $x$ units per week at price $p = 120 - 2x$ dollars. Revenue is $R = xp$. Find the
output and price that maximise revenue, and the maximum revenue. State the units.

**32.** Prove Vieta's formulas: if $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ with $a \neq 0$ has roots $r_1$ and $r_2$, then
$r_1 + r_2 = -\dfrac{b}{a}$ and $r_1r_2 = \dfrac{c}{a}$.

## Challenge

**33.** The equation $x^2 + bx + c = 0$ has rational coefficients and one root $2 + \sqrt{5}$. Find
$b$, $c$ and the other root, and prove that the other root must be $2 - \sqrt{5}$.

**34.** Find every real $k$ for which $x^2 - 2kx + k + 6 = 0$ has two distinct real roots.

**35.** Solve $\left(x^2 - 2x\right)^2 - 11\left(x^2 - 2x\right) + 24 = 0$.

**36.** Let $x$ and $y$ be real numbers with $x + y = S$, where $S$ is a fixed real number. Prove that
$xy \leq \dfrac{S^2}{4}$, and determine exactly when equality holds. Then explain what this says about
the rectangle of largest area with a given perimeter.
