# Inequalities — Exercises

> [Tutorial](README.md) · 36 problems · Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Challenge

Work each problem completely: show the algebraic steps, state the solution in interval notation, and
check at least one interior point and every boundary point. Write "no solution" or $\mathbb{R}$ when
the solution set is empty or all of the real line. Full solutions are in
[solutions.md](solutions.md); do not open that file until you have attempted the problems.

## Beginner

**1.** Write the set $\{x\in\mathbb{R}:-3\le x<5\}$ in interval notation.

**2.** Write the interval $(-\infty,2]$ as an inequality in $x$.

**3.** Solve $4x+7<23$.

**4.** Solve $5-3x\ge -7$.

**5.** Solve $\dfrac{x}{4}+2\le 5$.

**6.** Solve $-2(x-3)>8$.

**7.** Solve the compound inequality $2\le x+5\le 9$.

**8.** Solve the compound inequality $-1<3x-4\le 5$.

**9.** Solve $|x|\le 7$.

**10.** Solve $|x-3|<2$.

**11.** Solve $|x+1|\ge 4$.

**12.** Solve the system
$$\begin{cases}3x-2<7,\\ x+1\ge 0.\end{cases}$$

## Intermediate

**13.** Solve $3(2x-1)-4(x+2)\le 5x+6$.

**14.** Solve $\dfrac{2x-1}{3}-\dfrac{x+2}{2}>1$.

**15.** Solve $-4\le \dfrac{2-5x}{3}<6$.

**16.** Solve $|4x+1|\le 9$.

**17.** Solve $|5-2x|>1$.

**18.** Solve $2|x+3|-5<3$.

**19.** Solve $x^2-5x+6\le 0$.

**20.** Solve $x^2+2x-8>0$.

**21.** Solve $\dfrac{x-3}{x+2}\ge 0$.

**22.** Solve the system
$$\begin{cases}3x-1<8,\\ 2x+5\ge 1.\end{cases}$$

**23.** A machined rod is acceptable when its length $L$ (in millimetres) satisfies
$|L-75|\le 0.05$. Find the interval of acceptable lengths.

**24.** A taxi charges a fixed fee of $\$3$ plus $\$2$ per kilometre. A passenger has at most
$\$25$ and must travel at least $4$ km. Find the possible distances $d$ in kilometres.

## Advanced

**25.** Solve $\dfrac{x-1}{x+4}\le 1$.

**26.** Solve $x^2\ge 2x+3$.

**27.** Solve $|x-1|<|2x+1|$.

**28.** Solve $\dfrac{1}{x-2}\ge \dfrac{1}{x+3}$.

**29.** Solve $(x-2)^2>0$.

**30.** Solve $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-1}\ge 0$.

**31.** Prove: if $0<a<b$, then $a^2<b^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{a}>\dfrac{1}{b}$. State which order axioms or
theorems you use at each step.

## Challenge

**32.** Solve $|x-3|+|x+1|<6$.

**33.** Solve $\dfrac{1}{x-1}+\dfrac{1}{x-2}\ge 1$.

**34.** Solve $|x^2-4|<3$.

**35.** Prove the triangle inequality: for all real $x,y$,
$$|x+y|\le |x|+|y|.$$
Then determine exactly when equality holds.

**36.** Solve
$$|x-1|+|x-2|+|x-3|\le 4.$$
